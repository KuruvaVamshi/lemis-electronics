Subsystem 01 • Hydrology

Subterranean Rainwater Percolation Vaults

100% of rainwater runoff from rooftops and paved avenues is channeled into deep percolation wells that recharge local subterranean aquifers. Dual-plumbing infrastructure recycles greywater for lush native landscaping.

Subsystem 02 • Clean Energy

Monocrystalline Rooftop Solar Microgrids

Each villa features high-efficiency 5.5kW monocrystalline photovoltaic arrays paired with smart grid inverters, generating over 7,200 kWh of clean renewable energy per year and powering common area lighting.

Subsystem 03 • Thermal Physics

Bioclimatic Aerodynamic Venting & Cavity Walls

Porotherm terracotta cavity masonry creates thermal dead-air pockets. Combined with prevailing wind corridor alignment, indoor ambient temperatures stay 5°C to 6°C cooler without artificial HVAC load.

Subsystem 04 • Structural Longevity

Fe-550D TMT Steel Core & M35 Ready-Mix Concrete

Engineered for 100+ years of resilience. Primary high-elongation steel rebars and M35 batching concrete surpass Indian Standard safety factors by 18%, verified by ultrasonic pulse velocity audits.
